About Us

Engineering India's Highways, Bridges & Expressways

M/s. Aerotide Infrastructure Engineering Pvt Ltd was established in 2017 by Mr. Vishwanath Bangari and Mrs. Revati Prakashgoud Bantanal. We are a hand-picked team of experienced consultants in the field of infrastructure and transportation, combining deep individual expertise with a dynamic multi-disciplinary approach to deliver industry-leading planning, design, and outcomes on complex road infrastructure projects.

The company launched its Bengaluru branch office in 2020 to expand its base, and today operates from offices in Bijapur, Mumbai and Bengaluru.

  • Sustainable transportation infrastructure through value engineering
  • Reducing infrastructure cost via simple, effective design principles
  • Innovative solutions to complex infrastructure challenges
  • Effective use of natural resources to minimise environmental impact
